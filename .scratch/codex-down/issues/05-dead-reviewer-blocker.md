# 05: A dead reviewer becomes an open blocker

**What to build:** after N failed Codex attempts in a row (N in the policy module), the boss's tracker gets an open blocker "Codex down" with a ladder marker. It escalates on the normal rungs without a boss turn, and it clears after M answered Codex attempts in a row. Today an outage leaves every PR waiting with nothing telling the boss what to do; this makes it an open blocker the ladder already knows how to raise. How the owner is asked, and what the owner's answer allows, is 09.

**Scope:** amended 2026-10-04 after Codex review 1 of the plan (finding 7), the owner-accepted premortem (R21 to R24, R25) and Codex review 3 (findings 4 to 7, 23). Review 3 split the owner's choice and the waiver out as 09. Blocked by 02 and 08: the blocker's `ask=` text offers "accept the fallback review", which does not exist before 08 (and 03, which 08 builds on, is behind it). Health is judged from real review attempts only: there is no synthetic Codex probe, so a recovered Codex is noticed at the next review or at 10's recheck pass, and the blocker stays open until then. 05 also defines the `tracker.update` and `tracker.add_line` contracts that 06 and 10 rely on.

**Blocked by:** 02 (PR review becomes a command), 08 (The fallback review, labelled and provisional)

The tracker writer (R23, finding 5):

- [ ] `tracker.py` gains `update(path, fn, deadline)` and `add_line(path, title, line, tag, deadline)`. `update` takes the sibling lock from `tracker.lock_path` with `flock` until `deadline` (a `time.monotonic()` value, as in `tracker_add` today), reads the file, calls `fn(text)` for the new text or `None` for no change, and writes by temp file and rename with the file mode kept, retrying up to 3 times when the file's stat changed under it. It returns `"done"`, `"done (no change)"` (`fn` returned `None`) or `"failed: <reason>"` (the lock held past the deadline, the file changed 3 times, or the name of an `OSError`), and never raises for those. `add_line` is the body of `tracker_add` in `boss-jev.py` moved over, generalised to a section `title`: it adds `line` under that section (created when missing, by `tracker.insert_in_section`) unless `tag` is already in the file, in `update`'s one locked step, and returns what `tracker_add` returns today (`"done"`, `"done (already there)"`, `"failed: ..."`). `boss-jev.py`'s `tracker_add` keeps its two path checks (`in_pm`, `regular_mine`) and calls `add_line`; `test_boss_jev.py` passes unchanged. Tests: each return value of `update`, and eight concurrent processes calling `add_line` with one tag give one line. 06 and 10 reuse these two functions
