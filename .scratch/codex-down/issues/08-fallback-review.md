# 08: The fallback review, labelled and provisional

**What to build:** when Codex does not answer, `boss_review.py` runs the fallback reviewer that 03 confined, and records its verdict as a **provisional** GO or NO-GO. The comment says it is a fallback review and why Codex was not there. A provisional GO lets a merge go ahead only on a branch the repo marks non-deploying; 04 enforces that. This ticket also replaces the skill's two absolute merge sentences with one rule that covers every kind of GO the track can now produce.

**Scope:** updated on 2026-11-12 (Solveig, task #08S) to the interface that 03, 07 and 13 have or will have on `main`; every box changed that day carries a `(2026-11-12 ...)` note saying what moved. New on 2026-10-04, split out of the original 03 after Codex review 3 of the plan (findings 2, 3, 20, 23), with the owner-accepted premortem (R10, R11, R12, R13, R25). 08 adds the `provisional` value to the round record's `kind` (02 writes `codex`; 09c adds `waived`). The fallback is passed to 01's chain with 03's `fallback_spec()` through 03's `fallback_flags(...)`, so brief, fence, findings and redaction are 07's (merged: `build_brief`, `findings_block`, `redact`, `comment_body`) and the confinement is 03's (`extra_args`, `env_unset`, `--restricted`, `--safe-mode`). Plan decision 1 says the fallback runs when Codex does not answer; `coverage.md` lists the consequence for R12 as a residual risk (authentication, quota and CLI errors also run it, and are labelled, not prevented). The fallback has no command-line switch until 06 lands (the switch moved from 08 to 06 in October; 06's box "No track, no provisional review" sets `allow_fallback` from `main()`, and this ticket leaves `main()` alone; confirmed in spec round 1, 2026-11-12): `review()` takes `allow_fallback` (default false) and `main()` never sets it, because a provisional GO must be written to 06's durable list before it is posted and that list does not exist yet; 06 then sets it from the command line where a usable `--track` is given (no track, no fallback). Until 06 lands a Codex outage therefore still stalls the PR gate, and the track pauses (premortem R1).
